2. This website

This is a static information and verification site. It sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. When you visit, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc., USA) processes technically necessary server logs (IP address, time, requested file) to deliver the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The evidence verifier runs entirely in your browser; verified files are never uploaded.

3. The CarProof app

The app works locally on your device, with no account and no cloud. Nothing is transmitted to us or to third parties by default. The app contains no ads and no tracking.

Data processed on the device: photos of vehicle handover and return, optional GPS location, device and capture metadata, optional voice notes (currently iOS only), vehicle and contract data, signatures, and an optional planned return time for the local reminder notification. Permissions (camera, optional location, optional notifications) can be denied or revoked at any time.

All data lives exclusively in the app container on your device. Delete it at any time via "Delete all data" in the app or by uninstalling. The app deliberately keeps its evidence out of cloud backups.

Data leaves your device only on your action: when you actively share a report or evidence package, when the optional trusted timestamp (iOS) sends a single 32-byte hash to the time-stamping authority you chose (never photos or personal data), or when the optional claim webhook (iOS) sends the machine-readable claim without photos to a system you configured.

The one-time Pro purchase is handled entirely by the store: Google Play Billing on Android, App Store in-app purchase on iOS. We receive no payment data and create no user account; entitlement is checked on the device against the store.

4. Third-party personal data

Evidence photos may contain license plates or bystanders. Processing for evidence purposes can be lawful under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; check necessity before passing anything on.

5. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR) and may complain to a supervisory authority. Since app data is stored only on your device, you exercise access and erasure directly in the app.
